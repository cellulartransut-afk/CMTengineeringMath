> **Template note (delete before printing).** For small jobs with a clear scope, up to about B$10,000: visible repairs, painting, door or window swaps, shutters, small decks, fixtures. If you can't see the problem until you open it up, use `time-and-materials-repair-agreement.md`. Over about B$10,000, or anything with drawings or structural work, use `owner-contract-small-works.md`. Fill every `[ ]` or write N/A. ## Page 2: Standard Terms

1. **Scope.** We do only the work listed on page 1. Anything else is extra and needs a Change Order under term 6.
2. **Payment.** You pay by the schedule on page 1. We order materials and start only after the deposit clears.
3. **Late payment.** Overdue sums carry interest at [1.5]% per month from the due date until paid. You also pay our reasonable costs of collecting overdue sums, including legal costs.
4. **Right to stop work.** If a payment is [7] days overdue, we may stop work after [3] days' written notice until it is paid. Time is extended by the stoppage, and you pay our reasonable costs of stopping and restarting.
5. **Materials stay ours until paid.** Materials delivered to site remain our property until paid for. If you do not pay, we may collect any materials not yet built in.
6. **Changes.** We do not do extra or changed work on verbal instructions. A change is valid only when its price and time are agreed in writing before the work starts: a Change Order signed by both of us or, for a change under B$[500], our WhatsApp or email message stating the change, price and time and your written reply approving it.
7. **Hidden conditions.** If we find a problem that could not be seen or expected when we priced (rot, termites, mould, corroded steel, faulty existing wiring or plumbing, rock, buried pipes or debris), we stop the affected work, show you photos and price the fix. The fix is extra. In an emergency we do only what is needed to make safe or keep water out, tell you the same day and price it within 48 hours.
8. **Time extensions.** The finish date moves by a fair period for delay caused by: weather, tropical storms and hurricanes, including watches, warnings and clean-up; your changes, late decisions, late payments or other contractors; permit, inspection or utility delays not caused by us; shipping, customs or supplier delays outside our control; hidden conditions; or anything else outside our reasonable control. We tell you in writing within [7] days of knowing about a delay.
9. **Your part.** You give us access during working hours, [provide water and electricity,] clear contents and valuables from the work area, tell us about known problems or hazards, and do not instruct or pay our workers or subcontractors directly. Items you supply are your responsibility for timely delivery and defects. We warrant our installation only.
10. **Standards.** We work in a good and workmanlike manner, to the Bahamas Building Code and manufacturers' instructions. Licensed persons do electrical and plumbing work. We may use subcontractors and remain responsible for their work.
11. **Warranty.** We warrant our workmanship for 12 months from completion and will fix defects you report in writing in that time. Manufacturers' warranties pass to you. Not covered: normal wear; misuse or lack of maintenance; damage from storms, floods or other events outside our control; work by others; items you supplied; existing parts of the building we did not work on.
12. **Limit of liability.** Our total liability under this agreement is limited to the Price. We are not liable for loss of rent, profit or use, or other indirect loss. This does not limit liability for death or personal injury caused by our negligence, or for fraud.
13. **Insurance.** We carry public liability insurance. You keep your building and contents insured, including against windstorm, while we work.
14. **Completion.** When we finish, we tell you and we walk the work together within [3] days. We fix punch list items within [7] working days. Punch list items do not delay the completion payment, except you may hold back B$[ ] until they are done.
15. **Ending the agreement.** Either of us may end this agreement if the other seriously breaks it and does not put it right within [7] days of written notice. If it ends for any reason other than our breach, you pay for work done, materials ordered or delivered, and our reasonable costs of leaving site. If it ends for our breach, you pay for work properly done, less the reasonable extra cost of finishing it.
16. **Disputes.** We first try to settle any dispute by meeting within [7] days of a written request. If it is not settled within [14] days, either of us may [refer it to mediation in Nassau and then] bring proceedings in the courts of The Bahamas. Nothing stops either of us claiming a sum that is due and not disputed.
17. **Law.** This agreement is governed by the laws of the Commonwealth of The Bahamas.
18. **Notices and photos.** Notices must be in writing. Email or WhatsApp to the contacts on page 1 counts. We may photograph the work for our records and, with your consent, for marketing without showing your address.
19. **Whole agreement.** Page 1, these terms, the special conditions and any signed Change Orders are the whole agreement. They replace earlier quotes and discussions.
