# Maintenance Service Agreement

Rev 1 · 2026-11-04

> **Template note.** For recurring maintenance of homes, rental units, villas, small commercial units and common areas, for landlords, property managers, gated communities and short-term rental owners, including owners who live abroad. The client pays a monthly fee in advance for scheduled inspections and a set number of hours of minor work. Everything else goes on a Work Order. Any project over the limit in clause 5.3 goes on its own short-form or small works agreement. Fill every `[ ]` or write N/A. Have a Bahamian attorney review this template once before first use.

## 1. Properties and Term
1.1 This Agreement covers the properties listed in Schedule 1 ("**the Properties**").
1.2 It runs for 12 months from the start date ("**the Term**").
1.3 It renews automatically for further 12-month terms unless either party gives written notice at least 30 days before the end of the current term.
1.4 The Contractor may change the fee or rates for a renewal term by telling the Client in writing at least [60] days before the term ends. If the Client does not accept the change, it may give notice under clause 1.3.

## 2. Included Services
2.1 For the monthly fee, the Contractor provides for each Property:
(a) scheduled inspections at the frequency in Schedule 1, using the checklist in Form B;
(b) minor items up to the included hours per month in Schedule 1;
(c) [AC filter changes every [ ] months; filters charged under clause 2.5 / included];
(d) a written report with dated photos after each visit (clause 8);
(e) a baseline inspection of each Property within [30] days of the start date (clause 10.1).
2.2 **Minor items** are small repairs and adjustments one person can do in about [1] hour each with common materials, for example: adjusting doors, hinges, locks and cabinet doors; replacing bulbs, smoke alarm batteries, tap washers, toilet flappers and fill valves; clearing sink blockages with a plunger or hand snake; re-sealing small areas; minor touch-up painting; clearing gutters on single-storey buildings.
2.3 Work that must be done by a licensed electrician or plumber is done by one and is outside the included hours unless Schedule 1 says otherwise.
2.4 Unused hours [do not carry over / carry over to the next month only]. Hours above the included hours are charged at Schedule 2 rates, within the limits in clause 6.
2.5 Materials used for minor items are charged at cost plus [ ]% [, except consumables up to B$[ ] per month, which are included].

## 3. Fees and Payment
3.1 The monthly fee is paid in advance by the [1st] of each month. The first month's fee is paid on signing. Services start when it clears.
3.2 Work Orders, extra hours and materials are invoiced [monthly with the statement / on completion] and are due within [7] days.
3.3 Fees are in Bahamian dollars. Payment in US dollars is accepted at par (1:1). The Client pays all bank, wire and card fees so the Contractor receives the full amount.
3.4 VAT is [added at the prevailing rate, currently 10% / not charged: the Contractor is not VAT registered].
3.5 Payment by [bank transfer / standing order / online card payment] to [ ].

## 4. Response Times

| Priority | Examples | We respond within | Someone on site within |

4.1 "Respond" means a call or message back confirming the request. "On site" means a person arrives to assess and make safe. The full repair may need parts or another visit.
4.2 Emergency call-outs outside normal hours are charged at Schedule 2 rates [unless Schedule 1 includes them].
4.3 Response times do not apply: during a tropical storm or hurricane watch or warning and the recovery after it (clause 9 applies instead); during island-wide power or water outages; when roads or the Property cannot be safely reached; when access has not been arranged; or where parts or materials must be shipped in. For Family Island Properties, add [ ] to each response time.
4.4 For fire, gas leaks, injury or crime, call emergency services (919 or 911) first. For downed power lines, call BPL. Then call us.

## 5. Work Outside the Plan
5.1 Work not included in clause 2 is done only under a written Work Order (Form A), at Schedule 2 rates or at a fixed price stated in the Work Order.
5.2 A Work Order is approved when the Client or its representative signs it, or approves it by WhatsApp or email. Work within the limits in clause 6.1 is approved by this Agreement.
5.3 Any single project over B$[ ] is quoted separately and done under its own agreement.

## 6. Spending Approval
6.1 The Contractor may spend up to B$[ ] per item (labour and materials) without asking first, to fix a reported problem or prevent damage, up to a total of B$[ ] per month for all Properties.
6.2 Above those limits, the Contractor needs the Client's written approval before starting. WhatsApp or email is enough.
6.3 **Emergencies.** If there is an emergency and the Contractor cannot reach the Client or its representative within [2] hours, it may spend up to the emergency make-safe limit per event to stop water, secure openings, isolate power, tarp or board up. It reports this the same day.
6.4 Every amount spent under clauses 6.1 and 6.3 is shown in the next report, with photos and receipts.

## 7. Keys, Access and Occupants
7.1 The Client provides keys, codes, gate passes and alarm details for each Property and keeps them up to date.
7.2 The Contractor keeps keys locked away, tagged by a code and not by address, logs each use (date, person, reason), and keeps codes confidential.
7.3 The Contractor is not responsible for loss or damage caused by anyone else who holds keys or codes. If a key is lost through the Contractor's fault, the Contractor pays to re-key that lock.
7.4 The Client is responsible for giving tenants and guests any notice of entry their lease or booking requires. The Contractor asks for routine visits [24] hours ahead through the Client. In an emergency the Contractor may enter without notice and will tell the Client right away.
7.5 For short-term rentals, the Contractor schedules visits between bookings using the calendar the Client shares. The Contractor does not handle guest check-ins, complaints, refunds or reviews. Damage caused by tenants or guests is charged as a Work Order.
7.6 Keys are returned within [5] days after this Agreement ends. The Client should then change codes.

## 8. Reports and Records
8.1 Within [2] working days after each visit, the Contractor sends a report by email or WhatsApp: the completed checklist (Form B), dated photos, work done, hours used, and items needing approval with a price or estimate.
8.2 Each month the Contractor sends a statement: fee, hours used against included hours, extra charges, Work Orders open and closed, and spending under clause 6.
8.3 Recommendations are advice. The Client decides. If the Client declines or delays a recommended repair, the Contractor notes it in the report and is not responsible for the result.
8.4 The Contractor keeps reports and photos for at least [24] months and gives copies on request.

## 9. Hurricane Preparation and Post-Storm Service
9.1 Hurricane season runs June 1 to November 30. This service is [included in the fee / a priority add-on at the fee in Schedule 1 / not taken].
9.2 **Before a storm.** When a tropical storm or hurricane watch or warning, or a forecast track, puts the island at risk, the Contractor carries out the prep list in Schedule 1 for each Property with this service.
9.3 The Client confirms the prep request at least [72] hours before forecast landfall. The Contractor's crew stops work about 48 hours before landfall to protect their own families. Prep that cannot be done after that point is not a breach.
9.4 **After the storm.** Once the authorities say it is safe and roads are open, the Contractor inspects each Property with this service within [48] hours, sends a damage report with photos for the Client's insurance, and makes safe within the limit in clause 6.3.
9.5 **Priority.** Clients with this service are served before others, in the order of the Contractor's priority list. In a major storm the Contractor cannot guarantee times. The safety of its people comes first.
9.6 Storm work is charged at the normal Schedule 2 rates. There is no storm surcharge. Materials are charged at actual cost plus the normal markup.
9.7 Prep reduces risk; it cannot prevent storm damage. The Contractor is not responsible for storm damage, except to the extent caused by its failure to carry out prep that was confirmed in time under clause 9.3, and then only within clause 12.

## 10. Pre-Existing Conditions and Items Not Maintained
10.1 The baseline inspection records the condition of each Property at the start, with photos. The Client receives a copy.
10.2 The Contractor is not responsible for:
(a) conditions that existed at the start date, whether or not found at the baseline inspection;
(b) items not listed as maintained in Schedule 1;
(c) failures caused by age, wear, manufacturer defects, design or original construction;
(d) damage by tenants, guests, other contractors, storms, floods, power surges or utility supply problems;
(e) problems that result from the Client declining or delaying recommended work.
10.3 Inspections are visual checks of areas that can be safely reached. The Contractor does not open walls or ceilings, move heavy furniture, test concealed pipes or wiring, or walk roofs that are unsafe. An inspection is not a home inspection, an engineering survey, a termite certificate, or a guarantee that no defects exist.

## 11. Warranty
11.1 The Contractor fixes its own faulty workmanship on minor items and Work Orders at no charge if it is reported in writing within [90] days of the work.
11.2 Projects done under a separate agreement carry the warranty in that agreement.
11.3 Manufacturers' warranties pass to the Client. The exclusions in clause 10.2 apply.

## 12. Limit of Liability
12.1 The Contractor's total liability for all claims under or in connection with this Agreement is limited to the fees paid by the Client under this Agreement in the 12 months before the event that gave rise to the claim.
12.2 The Contractor is not liable for loss of rent, booking cancellations, refunds or claims by guests or tenants, loss of profit or use, or other indirect loss.
12.3 This clause does not limit liability for gross negligence, wilful misconduct, fraud, or death or personal injury caused by negligence.

## 13. Insurance
13.1 The Contractor carries public liability insurance of at least B$[ ] and will provide a certificate on request.
13.2 The Client keeps the Properties and contents insured, including against windstorm, [and carries liability cover for the Properties].

## 14. Late Payment and Suspension
14.1 **Interest.** Overdue sums carry interest at [1.5]% per month from the due date until paid.
14.2 **Suspension.** If any sum is unpaid [7] days after its due date, the Contractor may suspend all services, including emergency response, on [3] days' written notice until paid. While services are suspended, the Contractor is not responsible for problems it would otherwise have found or dealt with.
14.3 **Costs of recovery.** The Client pays the Contractor's reasonable costs of recovering overdue sums, including legal costs.
14.4 **Title to materials.** Materials supplied remain the Contractor's property until paid for. The Contractor may remove any not yet installed.

## 15. Termination
15.1 Either party may end this Agreement at the end of a term by notice under clause 1.3.
15.2 Either party may end it early by [60] days' written notice.
15.3 Either party may end it by written notice if the other materially breaches it and does not remedy the breach within [14] days of written notice. The Contractor may also end it on [7] days' written notice if any sum is unpaid [30] days after its due date.
15.4 If the Client sells a Property, it may remove that Property from this Agreement on [30] days' written notice.
15.5 On ending: the Client pays fees to the end date, all work done and materials ordered; the Contractor returns keys and sends a final report within [5] days; fees paid in advance for any period after the end date are refunded within [14] days, unless the Agreement ended because of the Client's breach.

## 16. Disputes and Law
16.1 The parties will first try to settle any dispute by meeting, in person or by phone or video, within [7] days of a written request.
16.2 If it is not settled within [14] days, either party may [refer it to mediation in Nassau, by video if a party is abroad, and then] [bring proceedings in the courts of The Bahamas / refer it to arbitration in Nassau under the Arbitration Act of The Bahamas by a single arbitrator].
16.3 Nothing stops a party from claiming a sum that is due and not disputed.
16.4 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Commonwealth of The Bahamas. A Client living outside The Bahamas agrees that disputes will be decided in The Bahamas.

## 17. General
17.1 **Notices** must be in writing to the contacts above. Email and WhatsApp count. For a Client abroad, email to [ ] is the main notice address. A notice is received on the day sent if before 5 pm Nassau time on a business day, otherwise the next business day.
17.2 **Representative.** Approvals given by the Client's representative bind the Client, within the limit stated above.
17.3 **Confidentiality.** The Contractor keeps keys, codes, alarm details, tenant and guest information, and interior photos confidential. Photos are used only for reports unless the Client agrees otherwise in writing.
17.4 **Subcontracting.** The Contractor may use subcontractors and remains responsible for their work.
17.5 **Entire agreement.** This Agreement, its Schedules, the Forms and approved Work Orders are the whole agreement.
