# Notice Letters

Rev 1 · 2026-11-04

> **Template note.** For you, the contractor, to send to owners and subcontractors when something must go on record or something goes wrong. Clause numbers are from `owner-contract-small-works.md` (letters 1 to 9) and `subcontract-agreement.md` (letters 10 to 12). If the job is on a different agreement, swap in the clause numbers from the table below. Fill every `[ ]`. Have a Bahamian attorney review this template once before first use.

## How to send

- **Send in writing:** email or WhatsApp, plus a copy filed in the job folder. A phone call is not a notice. Follow every call with one of these letters.
- **Keep proof of sending.** Email: keep the sent item. WhatsApp: screenshot showing the date, time and delivery ticks, and export the chat at the end of the job. For letters 6, 7, 8 and 12B, also hand-deliver or courier a signed printed copy and get a signature or a photo of delivery.
- **Send to the notice contacts in the contract** (Small Works clause 17.3).
- **Send on time.** Letters 1, 2 and 4: the same day. Letter 3: within 7 days of knowing about the delay (clause 5.3). Payment letters: on the dates the contract allows, not before.
- **Firm, not rude.** Facts, dates, the clause, what you need and by when. No insults. No threats you won't carry out. Write as if a judge will read it, because one day one might.
- **Attach evidence:** dated photos, the invoice, the change order, earlier letters.
- **Log every letter:** date, letter number, to whom, how sent.
- Before sending letter 8 or 12B, call your attorney if the amount is large or the other side disputes the facts.

## Letter 1. Confirmation of verbal instruction ("as discussed today")

**Send when:** the same day the Owner, their architect or their representative asks you, on site or by phone, for something not in the written scope, or gives a decision you need on record (a selection, a location, an approval).
**Relies on:** clause 7 (changes), especially 7.1 (no change is valid without a signed Change Order).

**To:** [Owner name] **From:** [your name, company] **Date:** [ ]
**Re:** [project], [site]. Agreement dated [ ].

Dear [name],

As discussed today, [date, time], [on site / by phone], you asked us to:

[Exact description: what, where, size, material. Example: "move the kitchen sink 3 feet to the left and add one double outlet by the window."]

[Keep the paragraph that applies. Delete the others.]

[A. Priced now.] This is a change under clause 7 of our Agreement. Price: B$[ ] [plus VAT]. Time: adds [ ] working days. Change Order no. [ ] is attached. We will start this item once it is signed.

[B. Price to follow.] This is a change under clause 7. We will send you the price and time by [date]. We will not start this item until the Change Order is signed. [Work on [area] is on hold until then, and the completion date moves under clause 5.3.]

[C. No cost.] This does not change the price or the completion date. We are recording it so we both have the same note.

Please reply to confirm this is correct. If anything here is wrong, tell us today.

Regards,
[Name] · [Title] · [Company] · [phone]

*Short-form jobs: a change under the limit in term 6 is approved by the Owner's written reply. Ask them to reply "approved".*

## Letter 4. Hurricane and named storm notice

**Send when:** Part A when a tropical storm or hurricane watch, warning or forecast track puts the island at risk (about 5 days out). Part B after the storm, once you have inspected the site.
**Relies on:** clause 5.3(a) (extension of time for storms, watches, warnings and clean-up), 11.2 (Owner insures the building and contents, including windstorm), 12.3 (warranty does not cover storm damage).

### Part A. Before the storm

**To:** [Owner name] **Date:** [ ]
**Re:** [project], [site]. [Storm name].

Dear [name],

[Storm name] is forecast to affect [island] from about [date]. We are putting our hurricane plan into action at your site.

By [date and time, about 72 hours before forecast arrival] we will: secure or remove loose materials and debris; empty or remove the skip; lower or tie down scaffolding; cover open roof areas and fasten sheathing; install shutters on openings in our scope; photograph the whole site.

The site closes from [date and time]. Our crew is then released to protect their own families. Please do not enter the site while it is closed.

What we ask of you: secure your own items and contents; check that your property insurance, including windstorm, is in force (clause 11.2); [ ].

The storm, the watch and warning period, and the clean-up after it extend the Completion Date under clause 5.3(a). We will confirm the new date after we inspect.

Stay safe.
[Name] · [Company] · [phone]
