# Receipts and Releases

Rev 1 · 2026-11-04

> **Template note.** For you, the contractor: a receipt for every payment you take, a signed release for every payment you make to a sub, and a signed record at handover. Use Form 1 for every client payment, Forms 2 and 3 every time you pay a sub, and Forms 4 and 5 at practical completion. A release means the person signing gives up claims, so read it to them before they sign. Have a Bahamian attorney review this template once before first use, especially Forms 2 and 3.

**Rules for all forms**
- One copy to the other party, one in the job file. A clear photo by WhatsApp is fine as a first copy; file the signed original.
- Every payment in or out goes through the business bank account. No cash to workers or subs. If a client pays cash, count it in front of them and write the receipt on the spot.
- Number receipts in one running series and never reuse a number.
