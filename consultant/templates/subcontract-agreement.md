## 7. Quality and Inspection
7.1 Work to the drawings, specifications, the Bahamas Building Code and manufacturers' instructions.
7.2 The Subcontractor will not cover up work before the Contractor (and Building Control where required) has inspected it.
7.3 The Contractor may reject defective work in writing. The Subcontractor will fix it at its own cost within [48 hours / a time set in the notice].

## 8. Variations
No extra work is paid unless instructed by the Contractor in a written variation signed before the work starts. Instructions from the Owner directly are not variations; refer them to the Contractor.

## 9. Site Rules and Safety
9.1 The Subcontractor follows the Contractor's site rules and all laws on health and safety at work.
9.2 PPE (hard hat, boots, gloves, eye protection) at all times. Fall protection above [6] feet.
9.3 No alcohol or drugs on Site. No music that stops people hearing warnings.
9.4 The Contractor may remove from Site anyone who is unsafe, unqualified or abusive.
9.5 The Subcontractor reports every injury and near miss to the Contractor the same day and is responsible for its own workers, including NIB reporting for its employees.

## 10. Clean-up
The Subcontractor cleans its work area daily and removes its debris to the skip bin. If it doesn't after [24] hours' notice, the Contractor cleans and deducts the cost.

## 11. Backcharges
If the Subcontractor fails to perform any obligation, the Contractor will give written notice. If not remedied within [48] hours (or immediately for safety), the Contractor may do the work itself or by others and deduct the cost plus [10]% from sums due.

## 12. No Direct Dealing
During the Project and for [12] months after, the Subcontractor will not, without the Contractor's written consent, take instructions, payments or additional work on this Project from the Owner, or solicit the Owner for other work.

## 13. Warranty
The Subcontractor warrants the Subcontract Works for [12] months from practical completion of the Main Contract and will fix defects notified in that period at its own cost.

## 14. Indemnity and Insurance
The Subcontractor is responsible for, and will indemnify the Contractor against, claims for injury, death or damage caused by the Subcontract Works or the Subcontractor's people, except to the extent caused by the Contractor's negligence. The Subcontractor keeps the insurance in clause 4(c) in force throughout.

## 15. Termination
15.1 The Contractor may terminate by written notice if the Subcontractor: fails to start or keep up progress, does defective work and does not fix it, breaches safety rules seriously, or breaches clause 4(d) or 12; and has not remedied it within [3] days of written notice (immediately for serious safety breaches or clause 4(d)).
15.2 On termination for default, the Contractor may complete the Subcontract Works by others; the extra cost is deducted from sums due or recovered from the Subcontractor.
15.3 If the Main Contract is terminated, this Subcontract ends too, and the Subcontractor is paid for work properly done.

## 16. Disputes and Law
16.1 Disputes are first discussed in good faith within [7] days. If not resolved, [mediation then arbitration in Nassau under the Arbitration Act / the courts of The Bahamas].
16.2 This Subcontract is governed by the laws of the Commonwealth of The Bahamas.
