## 7. Changes
7.1 No change to the Works, Price or time is valid unless set out in a written Change Order signed by both parties before the changed work starts.
7.2 Emergency work needed to make the Site safe or prevent damage may be done first; the Contractor will notify the Owner the same day and issue a Change Order within 48 hours.
7.3 Changes are priced at the rates in the Contractor's estimate where applicable, otherwise at cost plus [ ]%.

## 8. Unforeseen Conditions
If the Contractor finds conditions that could not reasonably be seen or expected when pricing (including rot, termite damage, mould, corroded steel, defective existing wiring or plumbing, rock, buried debris or services), the Contractor will notify the Owner, stop affected work if needed, and propose a Change Order. Work to correct them is extra.

## 9. Price Escalation [for jobs over [8] weeks or with volatile materials]
If the supplier price of [steel, lumber, roofing, freight] rises more than [5]% between the date of this Agreement and the date of purchase, through no delay of the Contractor, the Contract Price is adjusted by the actual increase on proof of the supplier's invoices.

## 10. Standards, Permits and Inspections
10.1 The Contractor will carry out the Works in a good and workmanlike manner, to the drawings, the Bahamas Building Code and the manufacturers' instructions.
10.2 Electrical and plumbing work will be done by appropriately licensed persons.
10.3 The Contractor will arrange inspections required under the building permit.

## 11. Insurance
11.1 The Contractor will carry public liability insurance of at least B$[ ] and will provide a certificate on request.
11.2 The Owner will keep the existing building and contents insured, including against windstorm, during the Works.
[11.3 The Contractor / Owner will insure the Works against loss or damage (contractor's all risks), including named windstorm, to their full value.]

## 12. Warranty
12.1 The Contractor warrants its workmanship for **12 months** from practical completion ("**the Defects Period**"). The Contractor will repair defects in its workmanship notified in writing during the Defects Period, within a reasonable time.
12.2 Manufacturers' warranties on materials and equipment pass to the Owner.
12.3 The warranty does not cover: normal wear; misuse or lack of maintenance; damage by storms, floods or other events outside the Contractor's control; work by others; Owner-supplied items; existing parts of the building not worked on.

## 13. Owner-Supplied Items
Items supplied by the Owner are the Owner's responsibility for timely delivery, suitability and defects. Installation is warranted; the item is not. Late Owner-supplied items extend time.

## 14. Practical Completion and Handover
14.1 The Works are practically complete when finished except for minor items that do not prevent use. The parties walk the Works and agree a punch list within [3] days of the Contractor's notice.
14.2 The Contractor will complete punch list items within [10] working days. Punch list items do not delay payment of the completion stage, except the Owner may hold [B$[ ] / 2% of the Price] until they are done.

## 15. Termination
15.1 Either party may terminate by written notice if the other materially breaches this Agreement and fails to remedy the breach within [10] days of written notice.
15.2 On termination for any reason other than the Contractor's breach, the Owner pays for all work done, materials ordered or delivered, and reasonable demobilisation costs, plus [10]% of the unfinished balance as lost overhead.
15.3 On termination for the Contractor's breach, the Owner pays for work properly done, less the reasonable extra cost of completing it.

## 16. Disputes
16.1 The parties will first try to settle any dispute by meeting within [7] days of a written request.
16.2 If not settled within [14] days, either party may refer it to mediation in Nassau.
16.3 If not settled by mediation within [30] days, [the dispute will be finally decided by arbitration in Nassau under the Arbitration Act of The Bahamas by a single arbitrator agreed by the parties or, failing agreement, appointed as that Act provides / either party may bring proceedings in the courts of The Bahamas].
16.4 Nothing stops a party from suing for an unpaid sum that is due and not disputed.
