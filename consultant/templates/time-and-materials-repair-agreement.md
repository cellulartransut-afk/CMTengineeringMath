# Time and Materials Repair Agreement

Rev 1 · 2026-11-04

> **Template note.** For repairs where the cause or extent can't be seen until the area is opened up: roof and window leaks, rot, termite damage, hidden plumbing leaks, water damage, storm make-safe. The client pays actual time and materials at agreed rates, up to a cap the client controls. Once the problem is found and scoped, price the rest as a fixed price on the short-form or small works agreement if you can. Set the minimum call-out from your minimum job charge (`knowledge/06-money.md`). Fill every `[ ]` or write N/A. Have a Bahamian attorney review this template once before first use.

## 1. The Problem and the Work
1.1 Problem reported by the Owner: [e.g. "water stains on the master bedroom ceiling after rain; source unknown"].
1.2 The Contractor will investigate the cause, open up where needed, and repair it ("**the Work**"). Planned first steps: [e.g. "inspect roof and ceiling, remove damaged ceiling board, find the leak source, report with photos"].
1.3 The cause and extent cannot be seen until the area is opened up. That is why the Work is charged on time and materials and not at a fixed price. Any estimate given (B$[ ] to B$[ ]) is a guide, not a quote.
1.4 The Work does not include [painting beyond the repaired area; moving contents; [ ]] unless approved under clause 6.

## 2. Rates
2.1 Labour and equipment are charged at the rates in Schedule 1.
2.2 Materials are charged at cost plus [ ]%. Cost means the supplier's invoice price including delivery, freight, duty and brokerage, and VAT where the Contractor cannot claim it back. Receipts are attached to each invoice.
2.3 Subcontractors, specialist services, skip bins and dump fees are charged at their invoice plus [ ]%.

## 3. Not-to-Exceed Cap
3.1 The Contractor will not charge more than the cap in the Key Numbers ("**the Cap**") without the Owner's written approval. A WhatsApp message or email from the Owner, or from the Owner's contact named above, is written approval.
3.2 The Cap is a spending limit set by the Owner. It is not a fixed price, and it is not a promise that the Work will be finished within it.
3.3 **Early warning.** When charges reach [75]% of the Cap, the Contractor tells the Owner in writing what has been found, the cost to date and the estimated cost to finish.
3.4 **When the Cap is reached**, the Contractor will:
(a) stop work, leaving the area safe and as weathertight as reasonably possible;
(b) notify the Owner in writing the same day with the work done, cost to date, what remains, and a recommended new Cap (Form B);
(c) restart only when the Owner approves a new Cap in writing (signed Form B, or WhatsApp or email stating the new Cap).
3.5 **Emergency make-safe.** If stopping at the Cap would leave the property unsafe or open to water damage, and the Contractor cannot reach the Owner within [2] hours, the Contractor may do only the minimum make-safe work (tarping, temporary shoring, capping a pipe, isolating power), up to the pre-approval amount in the Key Numbers. By signing this Agreement the Owner approves that amount in writing, for this purpose only. The Contractor reports it the same day.
3.6 If the Owner does not reply to a request for a new Cap within [2] working days, the Contractor may leave the Site. Coming back later is charged as a new call-out.
3.7 If the Owner decides not to raise the Cap, the Work ends at that point and clause 15.3 applies.

## 4. Deposit
4.1 The Owner pays the deposit in the Key Numbers on signing. Work starts when it clears.
4.2 The deposit is [held as a retainer and applied to the final invoice / applied to the first invoice]. If the final account is less than the deposit, the Contractor refunds the difference within [7] days of the final invoice.

## 5. Timesheets, Photos and Records
5.1 The Contractor keeps a timesheet for each day worked (Form A): date, names and trades, start and finish times, work done, conditions found, materials and equipment used, and photo numbers.
5.2 Timesheets go to the Owner [daily / weekly with the invoice] by WhatsApp or email.
5.3 Dated photos are taken before opening up, of the conditions found, during the repair and on completion.
5.4 The Owner raises any query on a timesheet within [3] working days of receiving it. A timesheet not queried in that time is accepted for billing. A query does not delay payment of the undisputed part of an invoice.

## 6. Extra Work
6.1 Work outside clause 1 is done only with the Owner's written approval stating the work and its price or estimate. WhatsApp or email is enough.
6.2 Approved extra work is charged at the same rates and counts towards the Cap, unless the Owner raises the Cap at the same time.
6.3 No extra work is done on verbal instructions. The Contractor will confirm any verbal request in writing the same day.

## 7. Invoices and Payment
7.1 The Contractor invoices weekly, every [Friday], with timesheets and material receipts attached.
7.2 Each invoice is due within [5] days.
7.3 **Late payment.** Overdue sums carry interest at [1.5]% per month from the due date until paid. The Owner pays the Contractor's reasonable costs of recovering overdue sums, including legal costs.
7.4 **Right to suspend.** If an invoice is unpaid [7] days after its due date, the Contractor may suspend the Work on [3] days' written notice until paid, after leaving the area safe. Restarting is charged as a call-out.
7.5 **Title to materials.** Materials delivered to the Site remain the Contractor's property until paid for. If the Owner fails to pay, the Contractor may remove materials not yet built in.
7.6 Payment by [bank transfer to [ ] / cheque payable to [ ]]. No cash to workers or subcontractors.

## 8. Hidden Conditions and Existing Defects
8.1 Finding the cause and fixing hidden conditions is the Work. It is charged at the rates, within the Cap.
8.2 If the Contractor finds a condition that needs an engineer, a permit or much larger work than expected (for example structural damage, widespread termite damage, unsafe wiring), it will stop that part, notify the Owner in writing with photos and advise the next step. Engineers' and permit fees are paid by the Owner.
8.3 The Contractor is responsible only for its own work. It is not responsible for defects or damage that existed before the Work, including those it uncovers.

## 9. Time and Completion
9.1 The Contractor will start on [date / within [ ] working days after the deposit clears] and work with reasonable speed. There is no fixed completion date.
9.2 The Contractor is not responsible for delay caused by: weather, tropical storms and hurricanes, including watches, warnings and clean-up; late decisions, approvals or payments by the Owner; permit, inspection or utility delays; shipping, customs or supplier delays; hidden conditions; or anything else outside its reasonable control. It will tell the Owner in writing when such a delay happens.
9.3 When the Work is finished, the Contractor notifies the Owner in writing with completion photos and sends the final invoice. The Owner may inspect within [3] days and list any items to fix.

## 10. Owner's Responsibilities
The Owner will:
(a) give access to the Site during working hours and in emergencies;
(b) provide [water and electricity / not applicable];
(c) remove or protect contents and valuables in the work area;
(d) tell the Contractor about known problems, past repairs, hazards and buried services;
(e) reply to requests for approval within [1] working day, or within [2] hours in an emergency;
(f) arrange access with any tenants or guests;
(g) not instruct or pay the Contractor's workers or subcontractors directly.

## 11. Standards and Permits
11.1 The Contractor works in a good and workmanlike manner, to the Bahamas Building Code and manufacturers' instructions.
11.2 Electrical and plumbing work is done by appropriately licensed persons.
11.3 If the repair needs a building permit, [the Contractor will apply and the fees are charged at cost / the Owner will obtain it]. Work that needs a permit does not start until it is issued, except emergency make-safe.

## 12. Warranty
12.1 The Contractor warrants its workmanship on the repair it carries out for 12 months from completion.
12.2 **Patch repairs.** If the Contractor recommends a full repair and the Owner chooses a patch or partial repair, the Contractor will record this in writing, and the warranty on that item is limited to [30 days / none].
12.3 Temporary work (tarps, temporary shoring, temporary fixes) carries no warranty.
12.4 The Contractor does not warrant parts of the existing building or systems (roof, pipes, wiring) that it did not repair.
12.5 Manufacturers' warranties on materials pass to the Owner. The warranty does not cover normal wear; misuse or lack of maintenance; damage from storms, floods or other events outside the Contractor's control; work by others; or Owner-supplied items.

## 13. Limit of Liability
13.1 The Contractor's total liability under or in connection with this Agreement is limited to the total amount paid to the Contractor under it [or B$[ ], whichever is greater].
13.2 The Contractor is not liable for loss of rent, bookings, profit or use, or other indirect loss.
13.3 This clause does not limit liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, or for fraud.

## 14. Insurance
14.1 The Contractor carries public liability insurance of at least B$[ ] and will provide a certificate on request.
14.2 The Owner keeps the building and contents insured, including against windstorm. Any insurance claim for the damage being repaired is the Owner's to make. The Contractor will provide photos and a short report [at no charge / at the Schedule 1 rates].

## 15. Ending the Agreement
15.1 Either party may end this Agreement at any time by [2] days' written notice.
15.2 The Contractor may end it by written notice if an invoice is unpaid [14] days after its due date, or if the Site becomes unsafe to work on.
15.3 On ending, the Owner pays for all work done to the end date, materials ordered that cannot be returned (or the supplier's restocking fee), and the reasonable cost of making safe and leaving the Site. The deposit is applied to the final invoice. Any balance is refunded or invoiced within [7] days.

## 16. Disputes and Law
16.1 The parties will first try to settle any dispute by meeting within [7] days of a written request.
16.2 If it is not settled within [14] days, either party may [refer it to mediation in Nassau and then] bring proceedings in the courts of The Bahamas. Nothing stops a party from claiming a sum that is due and not disputed.
16.3 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Commonwealth of The Bahamas.

## 17. General
17.1 **Notices** must be in writing to the contacts above. Email and WhatsApp count. A notice is received on the day sent if before 5 pm on a business day, otherwise the next business day.
17.2 **Entire agreement.** This Agreement, Schedule 1, the Forms, and written approvals under clauses 3 and 6 are the whole agreement.
17.3 **Subcontracting.** The Contractor may subcontract parts of the Work and remains responsible for them.
17.4 **Photos** are for records and, with the Owner's consent, for marketing without showing the address.
