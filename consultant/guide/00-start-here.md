# Start here: the beginner's guide

Rev 1 · 2026-11-04

This guide explains everything in the consultant folder in plain words. It assumes you have never run a job, priced one, registered a business or heard most of the words. That's normal at 18. Nobody is born knowing what a tie beam or retention is. Learn it in order and it adds up fast.

## How the whole system fits together

Your business runs on five kinds of things (see `../operations-manual/00-index.md`):

1. **SOPs** (`../operations-manual/`): *how* you do each task, step by step.
2. **Contracts and forms** (`../templates/`): the paper each step uses.
3. **Spreadsheets** (`../tools/`): the math and the tracking.
4. **Knowledge** (`../knowledge/`): the *why* and the reference facts.
5. **Records** (`../my-business/` and your job folders): proof of what happened.

One job uses all of them, in this order:

## A 4-week learning plan

| Week | Read | Do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 00, 04, and the glossary as you go | Fill in `../my-business/profile.md`. Book the attorney or registered agent meeting. Pick your name |
| 2 | 03, 02 | Ask a working super if you can shadow on a site. Walk a job. Match what you see to file 03 |
| 3 | 05, 06 | Price a pretend job (your own family's bathroom) with the estimator. Fill a short-form agreement for it |
| 4 | 07, and SOPs 01 to 06 | Build your sub bench: two subs per trade, documents checked. Log them in office-tracker Subs & Documents |

## When you don't understand something

Ask the consultant, in plain words: "I don't get what retention is", or "Explain the before-pour checklist like I've never seen concrete." It will explain from first principles and give an example. No question is too basic. The expensive mistakes come from the questions people were too proud to ask.
