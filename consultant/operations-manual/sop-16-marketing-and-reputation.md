# SOP-16 Marketing and Reputation
Rev 1 · 2026-11-04 · Process owner: Owner

**Purpose:** Turn every finished job into the next two leads, and protect the name that makes people call you in a small country.

**When:** Weekly for posts and replies; at every closeout; monthly for referral partners and lead review; after every storm.

**Who:** Now: the owner. Later: office admin posts and answers messages; the owner keeps the referral partners, the reviews and every complaint.

**Forms and tools:**
- [office-tracker.xlsx](../tools/office-tracker.xlsx), Leads & Quotes (lead source column)
- [maintenance-service-agreement.md](../templates/maintenance-service-agreement.md) for repeat clients
- [01-roadmap.md](../knowledge/01-roadmap.md), best markets; SOP-13 closeout; Company/Marketing folder (SOP-15)

**Steps:**
1. Use one name, logo, phone and email on every channel: Google Business Profile, Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp Business, email signature, vehicle, site board.
2. Set up WhatsApp Business: hours, greeting and away messages, a catalogue of services, and labels for each lead stage.
3. Answer every message within 2 working hours (SOP-01). An unanswered message is a lost job and a bad story told at church on Sunday.
4. Photograph every job before, during and after, from the same angles. Shoot the hidden quality too: rebar checks, straps, membranes, flood tests. That is what sets you apart.
5. Post at least one job a week. No client names, faces or addresses without written consent (small works contract clause 17.4).
6. At closeout, ask for three things in the same week: a written testimonial, a Google review, and the names of two people who might need work. Send the review link by WhatsApp to make it easy.
7. Reply to every review within 48 hours. A bad review gets a calm, factual reply and an offer to fix it offline. Then fix it.
8. Keep a referral partner list: real estate agents, property managers, landlords, short-term rental owners, insurance adjusters. Visit or call two each month.
9. After every job for an agent or property manager, send them a short photo report. That report is what they show their own clients.
10. After a good job for a landlord, rental owner or property manager, offer a maintenance service agreement. Recurring work beats chasing leads.
11. Join the Bahamian Contractors Association and show up to meetings.
12. Review lead sources monthly in Leads & Quotes: which sources turn into paid jobs. Spend your time there.
13. Thank every referrer, in person or by message, the day the referred job is signed.

Rules:
- Say only what is true. Nobody is licensed under the Construction Contractors Act yet. Advertise your business licence number, and "insured" only while the policy is current.
- No hidden referral fees to an agent or manager who advises the client. If you ever pay one, the client knows in writing; ask your attorney first.
- After a storm: same margin as any other week, real price rises passed on with the supplier's invoice, existing clients first, honest dates. No gouging. People remember who helped them and who robbed them.
- Never run down a competitor. Never post a client's damage or a dispute.

**Done when:** Every closed job has photos, a testimonial and review request, and a referral ask logged; every review has a reply; two partner contacts happen every month.

**Records:** Company/Marketing: approved photos, testimonials, consent notes, referral partner list. Lead source in Leads & Quotes. Job photos stay in each job's 08 Photos.

**Mistakes this prevents:**
- Good work that nobody hears about.
- One angry client's story spreading with no reply from you.
- Posting a client's home without consent.
- Losing years of trust in one storm season of high prices.

**Review triggers:** A bad review or public complaint; lead sources changing for a quarter; the Contractors Board starts licensing (update what you may advertise); a new platform your clients actually use.
