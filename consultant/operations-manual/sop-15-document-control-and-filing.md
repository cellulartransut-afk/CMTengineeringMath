# SOP-15 Document Control and Filing
Rev 1 · 2026-11-04 · Process owner: Office Admin (the owner until hired)

**Purpose:** Every document lives in one place, named one way, backed up, and anyone can find it in two minutes, years later.

**When:** Every time a document is created, signed, received or photographed. Phone backup daily. Folder check at every monthly close (SOP-14).

**Who:** Now: the owner. Later: office admin keeps the system; the PM and superintendent file their own job documents the same day.

**Forms and tools:** A business cloud drive (Google Drive or OneDrive); a phone scanner app; [office-tracker.xlsx](../tools/office-tracker.xlsx), Leads & Quotes (job number register); [job-cost-tracker.xlsx](../tools/job-cost-tracker.xlsx) (one copy per job). Every other SOP files here.

**Steps:**
1. Use one cloud account owned by the business, with two-step login on. No business files on personal accounts or only on a phone.
2. Assign the next job number (YYYY-NNN, for example 2026-001) in Leads & Quotes when a site visit is booked (SOP-01). Never reuse a number. Lost quotes keep theirs.
3. Create the job folder the same day: Jobs/YYYY-NNN Client Short-Description Area, for example "Jobs/2026-001 Rolle Bathroom-Reno Carmichael". Put the job's copy of the tracker in its root as "2026-001 job-cost-tracker.xlsx". Create these subfolders:

| Subfolder | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| 01 Contract | Proposal, signed contract, client mark-ups, schedule, kickoff notes, contract notices, WhatsApp chat exports |
| 02 Estimate | Site sketch, estimate, supplier and sub quotes, client question list |
| 03 Permits and Drawings | Permit, current drawings, a Superseded subfolder, inspection results, site plan, hurricane plan |
| 04 Subs and POs | Scope sheets, bids, subcontracts, sub documents for this job, purchase orders, broker papers |
| 05 Change Orders | Signed change orders, pricing backup, sub variations, hidden-condition photos |
| 06 Invoices and Payments | Our invoices, receipts we issue, payment notices, supplier and sub invoices, sub receipts and releases |
| 07 Daily Reports | Daily reports, toolbox talk log, incident reports, weekly client updates |
| 08 Photos | One subfolder per day, named YYYY-MM-DD |
| 09 Closeout | Punch list, final inspections, certificate of occupancy, handover package, testimonial, final job cost, callback log |

4. Keep company records in Company/ with these folders: Legal and Licences (registration, business licence, sub master documents in a Subcontractor Documents subfolder), Insurance, Tax and NIB, Bank, Templates, Marketing. office-tracker.xlsx sits in the Company/ root.
5. Name every file: YYYY-MM-DD doc-type short-description. Examples: "2026-11-04 contract Rolle bathroom", "2026-11-18 change-order CO-02 extra outlet", "2026-11-20 invoice 2026-001-INV-03 rough-in". Numbers: change orders CO-01 per job; invoices 2026-001-INV-01; purchase orders 2026-001-PO-01.
6. Use these doc-types only: proposal, estimate, quote, contract, subcontract, sub-docs, permit, drawing, inspection, po, delivery-note, change-order, invoice, receipt, release, notice, daily-report, incident, punch-list, handover, letter.
7. Never overwrite a signed document. A revision gets "rev A", "rev B" at the end of the name. When new drawings arrive, move the old set to Superseded the same day and pull the old prints off site.
8. Scan signed paper to PDF the same day. Keep the originals in a waterproof box labelled with the job number, kept off the floor in hurricane season.
9. Turn on automatic camera backup on every work phone. At the end of each day, move that day's job photos into 08 Photos/YYYY-MM-DD.
10. Run one WhatsApp chat or group per job. WhatsApp is a notice channel under the contract, so export the chat, with media, into 01 Contract at the end of each month and at closeout. Start every email subject with the job number.
11. Once a month, copy the whole drive to an external drive kept away from the office. Before every storm, confirm the drive has synced (SOP-12).
12. Give staff access only to the folders they need. Remove access the day someone leaves.
13. Keep job files at least 7 years after the job closes, and keep the licensing evidence (contract, permit, photos, final invoice, proof of payment, client letter) permanently. Confirm the legal retention period with your accountant and attorney. Never delete a job folder early.

**Done when:** Every active job has a complete folder, every file follows the naming rule, phones are backed up to the day, and the monthly offline copy exists.

**Records:** This SOP is the filing map for all records. The job number register is Leads & Quotes.

**Mistakes this prevents:**
- Losing a dispute because the signed change order is on a lost phone.
- Building from superseded drawings.
- Losing the whole business record in a storm, theft or a dead laptop.
- Having no evidence when the Contractors Board opens licensing.

**Review triggers:** A document could not be found when needed; a lost or stolen device; a new staff member; a change of cloud provider; attorney or accountant advice on retention.
