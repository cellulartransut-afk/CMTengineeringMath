# SOP-05 Purchasing and Materials
Rev 1 · 2026-11-04 · Process owner: Project Manager (the owner until hired)

**Purpose:** Buy the right materials at landed cost, get them on time, and never lose them between the yard and the wall.

**When:** The deposit has cleared (SOP-03), and every week after that as the look-ahead shows what is needed.

**Who:** Now: the owner. Later: the PM orders; the superintendent receives and checks deliveries; office admin matches invoices.

**Forms and tools:**
- [estimator.xlsx](../tools/estimator.xlsx), Landed Cost
- [job-cost-tracker.xlsx](../tools/job-cost-tracker.xlsx): Commitments, Actual Costs, Look-Ahead
- [04-estimating-and-materials.md](../knowledge/04-estimating-and-materials.md): lead times, local vs import, specs for salt, wind and termites
- [daily-report.md](../templates/daily-report.md), deliveries section

**Steps:**
1. Build the buy list from the estimate, by cost code, within 2 working days of the deposit clearing. Mark every long-lead item.
2. Order long-lead items the same week: impact windows and doors, trusses, AC units, generators, cabinets, special fixtures. Put each lead time on the Look-Ahead. Add 1 to 3 weeks for Family Islands.
3. Get written quotes with validity dates. Stock items: buy from a local yard. Big packages: get a local quote and a Florida quote landed on paper, then compare after lead time and risk.
4. Ask the client about duty concessions before you order. Concession paperwork goes in their name, done right, through a licensed customs broker.
5. Issue a written purchase order for every order, even by WhatsApp: PO number (for example 2026-001-PO-03), items, quantities, price, delivery date, site address. Log it in Commitments.
6. Once staff place orders, any PO over B$2,500 needs the owner's approval before it is sent.
7. Confirm stock and the delivery time in writing the day before delivery.
8. Check every delivery against the delivery note before you sign it. Count it, inspect it, and write any damage or shortage on the note. Photograph it. Reject wrong specs at the gate: beach sand, plain steel fasteners on a coastal job, untreated lumber where treated is specified.
9. Store materials off the ground, covered and locked. Deliver just in time. Copper, fixtures and tools never stay on site unsecured overnight.
10. Log owner-supplied items with their promised dates. Inspect them on arrival and report defects to the client in writing the same day. They are the client's risk.
11. Match every supplier invoice to its PO and signed delivery note before it goes for payment (SOP-11). Code it in Actual Costs.
12. Return surplus and wrong items inside the supplier's return window. Get a credit note.
13. If a supplier price rises more than the contract's escalation threshold, tell the client in writing, with the supplier's invoice, before you buy.
14. Family Islands: book freight early, pack for double handling, insure the load, and confirm the mailboat or barge sailing from Potter's Cay the week it goes.

Rule: no materials are bought for a client's job before that client's deposit clears.

**Done when:** Every item on the buy list is ordered, delivered, checked, stored, invoiced, matched and coded.

**Records:** 04 Subs and POs: quotes, POs, broker papers. 06 Invoices and Payments: supplier invoices and credit notes. 07 Daily Reports: delivery checks. 08 Photos: damaged or short deliveries.

**Mistakes this prevents:**
- Impact windows ordered late, stalling the job for three months.
- Paying for materials that never arrived or arrived broken.
- Theft from an open site.
- Wrong materials going into the work and failing in salt air or a storm.

**Review triggers:** A long-lead item arrives late; theft or loss on any site; a supplier dispute; a change in duty, freight or supplier terms.
