# SOP-04 Subcontractor Selection and Buyout
Rev 1 · 2026-11-04 · Process owner: Project Manager (the owner until hired)

**Purpose:** Get the right sub for each trade at the right price, under a signed subcontract, with documents on file, before they start.

**When:** Bid stage: the estimate needs a trade price (SOP-02). Buyout: the contract is signed and the deposit has cleared (SOP-03).

**Who:** Now: the owner. Later: the PM runs bids and buyout; the superintendent says who actually performs in the field.

**Forms and tools:**
- [subcontract-agreement.md](../templates/subcontract-agreement.md); [checklists.md](../templates/checklists.md) section 2 Vet a new subcontractor
- [job-cost-tracker.xlsx](../tools/job-cost-tracker.xlsx): Sub Bids, Commitments, Budget & Cost Report
- [office-tracker.xlsx](../tools/office-tracker.xlsx), Subs & Documents; [subs.md](../my-business/subs.md)
- [03-contracts.md](../knowledge/03-contracts.md), subcontract clause table

**Steps:**
1. Write a one-page scope for each trade: exact work, drawings and their dates, what we supply, what they supply, start window, crew size.
2. Send it to 3 subs. Use the bench first. Vet any new sub with checklist section 2 before they price: visit a job they are on now and call two contractor references.
3. Get every quote in writing. Log it in Sub Bids.
4. Level the bids: same scope, same exclusions, materials in or out, VAT in or out, duration. A low number with missing scope is not low.
5. Choose on value: shows up, quality, documents, then price. A bad sub costs more than a high price.
6. Within 5 working days of the deposit clearing, confirm scope, price and dates with the chosen sub.
7. Collect documents before signing: business licence, NIB letter of good standing, public liability certificate naming us, crew list confirming every person's right to work in The Bahamas, and a trade licence for electrical or plumbing. Log every expiry date in Subs & Documents.
8. Fill the subcontract: price, payment stages, 10% retention, schedule, minimum crew, no direct dealing with the client.
9. Sign before they start. No signature, no start. No exceptions for friends or family.
10. Enter the commitment in Commitments under its cost code, for example 09-30-S. Record the buyout gain or loss against budget.
11. Hold a pre-start meeting on site: scope, drawings, schedule, site rules, quality standard, how and when they get paid. Write it up the same day.
12. Give a new sub a small piece of work first. Give them more only after they pass.
13. Rate every sub 1 to 5 in subs.md when their work on the job is finished.

Rules: no advances. If a sub needs materials, we buy them or pay the supplier directly. No sub who uses undocumented workers. Spot-check the crew on site against the list.

**Done when:** Every trade on the job has a signed subcontract, current documents on file, a commitment logged, and a pre-start meeting recorded.

**Records:** 04 Subs and POs: scope sheets, quotes, bid levelling, signed subcontracts, document copies, pre-start notes. Master copies of each sub's documents in Company/Legal and Licences/Subcontractor Documents (SOP-15).

**Mistakes this prevents:**
- Hiring the cheapest number and paying twice to fix the work.
- An uninsured sub's injured worker becoming your claim.
- Scope gaps between trades that nobody priced.
- A sub walking off with an advance.

**Review triggers:** A sub fails or walks off; a document or insurance problem is found after a sub has started; your attorney changes the subcontract template.
