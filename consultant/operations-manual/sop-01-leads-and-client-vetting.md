# SOP-01 Leads and Client Vetting
Rev 1 · 2026-11-04 · Process owner: Estimator (the owner until hired)

**Purpose:** Answer every enquiry fast, log it, and spend estimating time only on clients who can and will pay.

**When:** Any enquiry: phone call, WhatsApp, email, Facebook or Instagram message, Google, a referral, or a government tender notice.

**Who:** Now: the owner. Later: office admin answers and logs the enquiry; the estimator makes the go or no-go call.

**Forms and tools:**
- [office-tracker.xlsx](../tools/office-tracker.xlsx), sheet Leads & Quotes
- [checklists.md](../templates/checklists.md), section 1 Vet a new client
- [01-roadmap.md](../knowledge/01-roadmap.md) (what work fits your stage); [02-bahamas-rules.md](../knowledge/02-bahamas-rules.md) section 9 (government work)

**Steps:**
1. Answer or reply within 2 working hours. Same day at the latest. Speed is the cheapest trust you can buy.
2. Log the lead in Leads & Quotes within 24 hours: date, name, phone, source, island and area, one-line scope, budget, wanted start date.
3. Ask the vetting questions on the first call (checklist section 1): who owns the property, who decides, who pays, budget, timeline, permit and drawings, any contractor fired on this job and why.
4. State your terms on that same call: written contract, deposit of 30% to 40% before work starts, payment by milestone. Note how they react.
5. Check fit: the work suits your current stage; it is no bigger than 3 times your largest completed job; it is above your minimum job charge ([06-money.md](../knowledge/06-money.md)); Family Island only if you will price the logistics in full.
6. Check the client on jobs over B$10,000: ask who referred them, ask an agent or supplier who knows them, and for new builds ask to see the conveyance or survey. A tenant needs the landlord's written permission.
7. Flag government and tender leads in the sheet. Read the tender documents for payment terms, bonds and required documents before you spend time pricing.
8. Decide go or no-go within 48 hours. Two red flags is a no. Refusing a deposit or wanting to skip the permit is a no on its own.
9. If go: book the site visit within 3 working days, confirm it by WhatsApp, and assign the job number and open the job folder (SOP-15).
10. If no-go: decline the same day, politely and in writing. Refer them to someone suitable if you can. Record the reason in the sheet.
11. Follow up any lead marked "waiting" every 7 days, three times. Then close it.

Red flags: refuses a deposit; wants to skip the permit; wants to pay cash with no invoice; has fired two or more contractors on this job; the decision-maker is never available; "start tomorrow, paperwork later"; argues price before you have priced.

**Done when:** Every enquiry is in Leads & Quotes with a status (site visit booked, declined, waiting or closed) and a dated go or no-go decision.

**Records:** Leads & Quotes row for every enquiry. If go, call notes and any photos the client sent go into the new job folder, 02 Estimate (SOP-15).

**Mistakes this prevents:**
- Losing work because nobody answered the phone.
- Spending a week estimating for a client who was never going to pay a deposit.
- Taking a job too big, too far away, or too small to make money.

**Review triggers:** Win rate outside 25% to 40% for a quarter; a client turns out to be a non-payer that vetting should have caught; the business moves to the next stage in the roadmap.
