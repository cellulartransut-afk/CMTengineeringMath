# SOP-06 Job Start
Rev 1 · 2026-11-04 · Process owner: Project Manager (the owner until hired)

**Purpose:** Have everything in place before the first sub touches the site, so the job starts clean and stays that way.

**When:** The contract is signed and the deposit has cleared (SOP-03).

**Who:** Now: the owner. Later: the PM runs preconstruction; the superintendent sets up the site.

**Forms and tools:**
- [05-field-playbook.md](../knowledge/05-field-playbook.md): Preconstruction checklist, Running subs, Running the client
- [job-cost-tracker.xlsx](../tools/job-cost-tracker.xlsx): Job Info, Budget & Cost Report, Look-Ahead
- [02-bahamas-rules.md](../knowledge/02-bahamas-rules.md) section 7, permits and inspections
- [checklists.md](../templates/checklists.md); SOP-04, SOP-05, SOP-12

**Steps:**
1. Fill Job Info in the job's tracker the day the deposit clears. Load the budget from the estimate, line by line, by cost code.
2. Confirm the permit. If one is required, apply to Building Control (and Town Planning where needed) with a complete package and answer every query the same day. If none is required, get that confirmed in writing. Never start before the permit.
3. Get the current drawings, stamped where required. Read every sheet. Send questions to the architect or engineer in writing.
4. For new work, locate the survey pegs and confirm setbacks. Before any digging, locate existing water, power, septic and cable.
5. Buy out the subs (SOP-04) and order long-lead items (SOP-05).
6. Build the schedule and the three-week look-ahead. Send both to the client and the subs.
7. Make a one-page site plan: deliveries, storage, skip bin, toilet, parking, security, neighbours.
8. Confirm insurance: your public liability is current; contractor's all risks if the job needs it; named-storm cover; the client has insured the existing building (small works contract clause 11).
9. If any work falls between June 1 and November 30, write the job hurricane plan now (SOP-12).
10. Photograph and video the existing conditions, inside and out, before anything moves. Send the client a copy. This settles "you damaged it" arguments.
11. Hold the kickoff meeting with the client: one contact on each side, how changes work, selection deadlines, payment dates, weekly update day. Send written notes within 24 hours.
12. Tell the neighbours the start date, work hours and your phone number.
13. Set up the site: first aid kit, emergency contacts, site rules and permit posted, toilet, skip bin.
14. Hold the pre-start meeting with each sub before their first day (SOP-04).
15. Walk the site with the first sub on day 1. Start the daily report that day (SOP-07).

**Done when:** Every item on the Preconstruction checklist in 05-field-playbook.md is ticked and dated, and the client has the schedule and the kickoff notes.

**Records:** 01 Contract: schedule, kickoff notes. 03 Permits and Drawings: permit, drawings, queries and answers, site plan, hurricane plan. 08 Photos: existing conditions. Job Info and budget in the job's tracker in the job folder.

**Mistakes this prevents:**
- Starting without a permit and getting a stop order.
- A client claiming you caused damage that was already there.
- Subs arriving before materials, or windows arriving after the walls are finished.
- A client who never agreed how changes and payments work.

**Review triggers:** A job start delayed by anything on this list; Building Control changes its submission process; a new kind of job (new build, Family Island, commercial).
