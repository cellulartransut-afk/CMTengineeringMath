# SOP-14 Bookkeeping, Monthly Close and Taxes
Rev 1 · 2026-11-04 · Process owner: Office Admin (the owner until hired; the accountant reviews)

**Purpose:** Know where every dollar is, keep tax money untouched, and file everything on time.

**When:** Weekly bookkeeping; tax transfers every time money comes in; monthly close by the 10th of the next month; yearly items on their due dates.

**Who:** Now: the owner, with a Bahamian accountant at least once a year. Later: office admin keeps the books and runs the close; the owner reviews and signs it off.

**Forms and tools:**
- [office-tracker.xlsx](../tools/office-tracker.xlsx): Monthly Money, VAT Threshold, Receivables, Subs & Documents
- [job-cost-tracker.xlsx](../tools/job-cost-tracker.xlsx), Budget & Cost Report (one per job); [cost-code-book.xlsx](../tools/cost-code-book.xlsx), Overhead Codes; [estimator.xlsx](../tools/estimator.xlsx), Labour Cost
- [06-money.md](../knowledge/06-money.md); [02-bahamas-rules.md](../knowledge/02-bahamas-rules.md) sections 3 to 6 and 11; [checklists.md](../templates/checklists.md) section 7
- Rates in use, below. Every one can change. Confirm with the named office before you rely on it.

| Item (from 02-bahamas-rules.md, as of 2026-11-03) | Figure | Confirm with |
|---|---|---|
| VAT | 10%. Registration mandatory once taxable supplies pass B$100,000 in any 12 months | Department of Inland Revenue, your accountant |
| NIB | Employer 6.65%, employee 4.65%, total 11.3% of wages up to B$810 a week (since 1 July 2024; new rates were expected from 1 July 2026, check). Return and payment by the 15th. Late penalty reported at 5% a month | National Insurance Board |
| Business licence tax | On prior-year gross turnover. Reported bands: exempt up to B$100,000; 0.5% above that to B$500,000; 0.75% above that to B$5M | Department of Inland Revenue |
| Minimum wage | B$260 a week, B$6.50 an hour | Department of Labour |

**Steps:**
1. Record every invoice, receipt and bank line in the accounting software within the week. Code job costs to job number and cost code (for example 09-30-S). Code overhead with the Overhead Codes sheet.
2. The day a client pays, move the tax money to the separate tax account. If VAT registered: VAT in a VAT-inclusive receipt = receipt x 10/110 (B$11,000 in = B$1,000 VAT). Once this year's turnover looks set to pass B$100,000, also reserve 0.5% of each receipt for next year's business licence tax.
3. If you have employees, on payday move their NIB to the tax account: your 6.65% plus the 4.65% you deducted. B$500 weekly wage = B$33.25 + B$23.25 = B$56.50.
4. Never spend from the tax account except to pay the tax.
5. Monthly close, by the 10th of the next month:
   - Reconcile the business and tax accounts to the bank statements.
   - All milestone invoices out; Receivables updated; late payers on the SOP-10 ladder.
   - Payables listed; subs and suppliers due are on the pay run (SOP-11).
   - Every job's Budget & Cost Report updated with forecast to finish. Margin within 2 to 3 points of estimate, or a written reason.
   - Overhead checked against budget.
   - Monthly Money filled in: cash, owed to us, we owe, revenue, gross profit, overhead, net profit, backlog in weeks.
   - VAT Threshold sheet updated with rolling 12-month taxable supplies. At B$75,000, book your accountant. Past B$100,000, register.
   - Tax account balance covers every tax owed.
   - Sub documents expiring in the next 30 days chased (Subs & Documents).
   - Owner paid the fixed salary only. No extra draws until reserves are full.
6. If an employer: file the NIB return and pay by the 15th.
7. If VAT registered: file the VAT return online and pay within 21 days of the end of each period (quarterly under B$5M turnover; reported, see 02-bahamas-rules.md section 4). File a nil return if you had no sales. Confirm your period at registration.
8. Pay your own self-employed NIB at the rate NIB gives you.
9. Yearly: renew the business licence with Inland Revenue, declaring last year's turnover (return reported due by 31 January, tax by 31 March; confirm each year, see 02-bahamas-rules.md section 3); pay the company's annual government fee to the Registrar General; renew insurance; get the NIB letter of good standing and Tax Compliance Certificate if bidding government work; in Freeport, renew the GBPA licence too. Give the accountant the year's books within 60 days of year end.
10. Put every filing and renewal date in the calendar with a 60-day reminder.

**Done when:** The month is closed by the 10th, the tax account covers every tax owed, and every return due that month is filed and paid.

**Records:** Company/Tax and NIB: returns, payment receipts, licence, certificates. Company/Bank: statements, reconciliations. Monthly Money and VAT Threshold in office-tracker.xlsx. Job cost reports in each job folder.

**Mistakes this prevents:**
- Spending the VAT and NIB, then having no money when they fall due.
- Passing the VAT threshold without registering.
- A profitable-looking month that hides a losing job.

**Review triggers:** Any rate, threshold or deadline change from Inland Revenue or NIB; VAT registration; the first employee; the accountant's yearly review.
