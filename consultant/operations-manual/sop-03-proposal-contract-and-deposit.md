# SOP-03 Proposal, Contract and Deposit
Rev 1 · 2026-11-04 · Process owner: Estimator (the owner signs)

**Purpose:** Turn the estimate into a signed contract and a cleared deposit before one dollar is spent on the job.

**When:** The estimate has passed SOP-02.

**Who:** Now: the owner. Later: the estimator writes the proposal and fills the contract; only the owner signs contracts until written signing limits are set.

**Forms and tools:**
- [quote-proposal.md](../templates/quote-proposal.md)
- [contract-guide.md](../templates/contract-guide.md) to pick the contract: [short-form-job-agreement.md](../templates/short-form-job-agreement.md), [time-and-materials-repair-agreement.md](../templates/time-and-materials-repair-agreement.md), [owner-contract-small-works.md](../templates/owner-contract-small-works.md), [maintenance-service-agreement.md](../templates/maintenance-service-agreement.md)
- [payment-application.md](../templates/payment-application.md) (deposit invoice); [receipts-and-releases.md](../templates/receipts-and-releases.md) (deposit receipt)
- [office-tracker.xlsx](../tools/office-tracker.xlsx), Leads & Quotes; [03-contracts.md](../knowledge/03-contracts.md)

**Steps:**
1. Write the proposal from the template: scope line by line, materials, exclusions, price, VAT status, payment stages, time, warranty.
2. Set the validity no longer than your supplier and sub quotes. 30 days maximum unless the contract has an escalation clause.
3. Deliver it in person or on a call. Walk the client through scope, exclusions and payments. Don't just send a PDF and hope.
4. Follow up on day 3, day 7, and 3 days before it expires. Log each follow-up in Leads & Quotes.
5. Negotiate scope, not margin. If the price comes down, scope or spec comes down with it, in writing.
6. When accepted, pick the contract with contract-guide.md. Use only templates your attorney has reviewed once. Fill every [ ]. Scope and exclusions match the accepted proposal word for word.
7. Check legal names: the client's full name matches the property owner or the person paying; your name matches your business registration. Company clients: company name and number.
8. If the client gives you their own contract, check it against the red flags list in 03-contracts.md. Mark up the changes in writing. Unusual terms go to your attorney before you sign.
9. Sign with the client: both sides, dated, witnessed. Give them their copy the same day.
10. Issue the deposit invoice the same day. Deposit is 30% minimum, normally 40%, and must cover materials and mobilisation.
11. Confirm the deposit has cleared in the business account. A cheque in hand or "I sent it" is not cleared.
12. Send a receipt within 24 hours of clearing. If VAT registered, move the VAT portion to the tax account the same day (SOP-14).
13. Mark the lead Won and start SOP-06. No orders, no subs mobilised, no start date confirmed until the deposit clears.
14. If you lose it: ask the client why, in one polite message. Record the reason in Leads & Quotes.

**Done when:** The contract is signed by both sides and filed, the deposit has cleared and been receipted, and the lead is marked Won.

**Records:** 01 Contract: signed proposal, signed contract, any client mark-ups. 06 Invoices and Payments: deposit invoice, bank confirmation, receipt.

**Mistakes this prevents:**
- Working on a handshake with no proof of scope or price.
- Buying materials with your own money for a client who then walks away.
- Signing someone else's contract that quietly moved the risk to you.

**Review triggers:** Attorney review of any template; a dispute that traces back to contract wording; clients regularly refusing a term you thought was standard.
