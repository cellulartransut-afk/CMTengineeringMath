# SOP-13 Closeout and Warranty
Rev 1 · 2026-11-04 · Process owner: Project Manager (the owner until hired)

**Purpose:** Finish clean, get paid in full, hand over a complete package, and treat every warranty call as a chance to keep the client for life.

**When:** The work is finished except minor items (practical completion), and then for the 12-month defects period.

**Who:** Now: the owner. Later: the PM runs closeout and warranty; the superintendent runs the punch list and callbacks; office admin runs the final billing and calendar.

**Forms and tools:**
- [checklists.md](../templates/checklists.md) section 6 Closeout; [05-field-playbook.md](../knowledge/05-field-playbook.md), Closeout checklist
- [owner-contract-small-works.md](../templates/owner-contract-small-works.md): clauses 12 (warranty), 14 (practical completion and handover)
- [receipts-and-releases.md](../templates/receipts-and-releases.md); [payment-application.md](../templates/payment-application.md)
- [job-cost-tracker.xlsx](../tools/job-cost-tracker.xlsx), Budget & Cost Report; [cost-code-book.xlsx](../tools/cost-code-book.xlsx), Unit Cost Book; [jobs.md](../my-business/jobs.md)

**Steps:**
1. Two working days before you call practical completion, walk the job with each sub and fix their items. Never show the client a job you have not punched yourself.
2. Notify the client in writing that the work is practically complete. Walk it together within 3 days (clause 14.1). Write the punch list; both sign it.
3. Book the final inspections. Get the certificate of occupancy where required and the BPL and Water & Sewerage connections.
4. Issue the completion invoice the same day as practical completion (SOP-10). Any punch-list holdback is only what the contract allows.
5. Finish the punch list within 10 working days (clause 14.2). The client signs off each item.
6. Final clean: inside, outside, skip bin gone, neighbours' side tidy.
7. Hand over the package: warranties, manuals, paint colours and product list, as-built notes, photos of what is inside the walls, keys, remotes and codes, how to operate the shutters, a short maintenance list.
8. Collect the final payment and send the receipt within 24 hours.
9. Pay subs their final payments against final releases (SOP-11). Release half their retention now; put the balance date in the calendar.
10. Take the final photos and ask for the testimonial, review and referrals (SOP-16).
11. Within 14 days of final payment: final job cost against estimate by cost code, final margin, Unit Cost Book updated, a row in jobs.md with the lesson.
12. Complete the licensing file: contract, permit, photos before, during and after, final invoice, proof of payment, client letter.
13. Calendar the warranty: a check-in call at 30 days and a walkthrough at 11 months, before the 12 months run out. Offer a maintenance service agreement at that walk ([maintenance-service-agreement.md](../templates/maintenance-service-agreement.md)).
14. Callbacks: acknowledge within 24 hours; attend within 3 working days; same day for active leaks, electrical hazards, or doors and locks that won't secure.
15. Decide the cause in writing. Our workmanship: fix it free. A sub's work: the sub fixes it under their warranty, or you fix and deduct from their retention. Misuse, storms, wear or other people's work: not warranty (clause 12.3); explain politely and quote it as new work.
16. At 12 months: release the remaining sub retention if no defects are open, and mark the job Closed in Leads & Quotes.

**Done when:** Punch list signed off, final payment received, subs paid and released, handover package delivered, job cost closed, and the warranty dates in the calendar.

**Records:** 09 Closeout: punch list, final inspections, certificate of occupancy, handover package, testimonial, final job cost, warranty callback log. 06 Invoices and Payments: final invoice, receipts, final releases.

**Mistakes this prevents:**
- A job that drags at 95% for months while the last payment waits.
- Paying a sub in full and then having nobody to fix their defect.
- Losing the lessons and real costs that make the next estimate right.

**Review triggers:** A final payment held up by closeout items; a warranty claim disputed; callbacks rising for two quarters; any change to the warranty clause.
