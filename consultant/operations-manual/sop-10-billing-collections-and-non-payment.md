# SOP-10 Billing, Collections and Non-Payment
Rev 1 · 2026-11-04 · Process owner: Office Admin (the owner until hired; the owner signs every notice from day 7)

**Purpose:** Bill on time, collect on time, and stop work before an unpaid client can hurt the company.

**When:** A payment milestone is reached; any payment is one day late; every Monday for the receivables review.

**Who:** Now: the owner. Later: the PM confirms the milestone is met; office admin invoices and sends the day 1 reminder; the owner makes the day 3 call and signs every notice from day 7 on.

**Forms and tools:**
- [payment-application.md](../templates/payment-application.md); [receipts-and-releases.md](../templates/receipts-and-releases.md)
- [notice-letters.md](../templates/notice-letters.md): reminder, intent to suspend, suspension, final demand
- [office-tracker.xlsx](../tools/office-tracker.xlsx), Receivables; [job-cost-tracker.xlsx](../tools/job-cost-tracker.xlsx), Billing
- [03-contracts.md](../knowledge/03-contracts.md); [02-bahamas-rules.md](../knowledge/02-bahamas-rules.md) section 12, getting paid

The Bahamas has no mechanics' lien law. You cannot lien the property. Your only security is the deposit, billing ahead of spend, the right to stop work, and title to materials. Use them early.

**Steps:**
1. Invoice the day the milestone is reached, not when the client asks. Attach dated photos and signed change orders, and bill approved changes on the same invoice.
2. Before it goes out, check the milestone meets the contract's definition and the numbers match the Billing sheet.
3. Send it by email and WhatsApp. Confirm the client received it. Log it in Receivables with its due date (small works template: 5 days from invoice).
4. When paid: confirm the money has cleared, send a receipt within 24 hours, move the tax money (SOP-14), and release sub payments for that work (SOP-11).
5. If a payment is late, run the ladder below. Count from the due date. Don't skip steps and don't jump ahead in anger. Facts, dates and amounts only.

| Day late | Action | Document |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Written reminder by WhatsApp and email: invoice number, amount, due date, interest clause | Reminder, notice-letters.md |
| 3 | The owner calls. Get a payment date. Confirm it in writing the same day. | Call note in the file |
| 7 | Notice of intent to suspend: work stops in 3 days if unpaid (small works clause 4.3). Order nothing more for the job. | Intent to suspend, notice-letters.md |
| 10 | Suspend. Secure the site. Stand the subs down in writing. Remove materials not yet built in (clause 4.4). Log suspension costs. | Suspension notice, notice-letters.md |
| 21 | Final demand: amount owed, interest, recovery costs, 7 days to pay, attorney next | Final demand, notice-letters.md |
| 30 | Hand the file to your attorney: letter before action, then court or arbitration as the contract says | Contract, invoices, notices, photos, daily reports |

6. Interest = overdue amount x contract rate x months late. At the template's 1.5% a month, B$10,000 one month late = B$150. Show it on the final demand.
7. If the client disputes part of an invoice, split it. Invoice the undisputed part now and insist it is paid (clause 16.4). Handle the rest under the disputes clause.
8. Payment plans only in writing, signed, with dated instalments. Work stays suspended until the account is current unless the owner decides otherwise in writing.
9. Restart after suspension only when paid in full, including suspension costs, with a new completion date in writing.
10. Every Monday, review Receivables. Target: private clients pay within 30 days on average.
11. Government jobs: flag them in Receivables and report their days outstanding separately. Expect slow payment. Submit invoices exactly as the contract requires, get written acknowledgement of receipt, and follow up in writing every 14 days. Don't suspend a government contract until your attorney has read its terms. Price the wait in the estimate and keep government under about a third of your work.

Rules: no cash taken without a receipt. No "discount for cash, no invoice". Never threaten; state the contract and the next step.

**Done when:** Every invoice is either paid and receipted, or sitting on a dated step of the ladder with the notice on file.

**Records:** 06 Invoices and Payments: invoices, bank confirmations, receipts, reminders, notices, call notes, payment plans. Receivables updated every Monday.

**Mistakes this prevents:**
- Working for weeks on a job that stopped paying.
- Losing the right to suspend or charge interest because notices were never sent.
- One slow government cheque starving the whole company.

**Review triggers:** Any debt reaching day 21; attorney advice on notice wording; a change in the contract payment terms; The Bahamas passing a lien or prompt-payment law.
